Who We Are

A club of young people, working for their village and beyond.

Youth Club Chauhag was formed by a small group of local youth who believed that real change starts at home — in the village, on the school road, at the health camp, and in the smallest acts of kindness toward neighbours. Today the club runs education, health, environment and welfare programs across Chauhag and neighbouring villages, powered entirely by volunteers.

Our Mission

To serve our village with dedication, responsibility and care.

With special thanks to the Himachal Government for encouraging youth-led service and community development.

We are building a cleaner, greener and stronger Chauhag through service in education, health, environment and community support.

Protect Nature

Tree plantation and cleanup drives for a greener village.

Educate Children

Support for learning, books and tuition where needed.

Serve Society

Helping families and neighbours whenever support is needed.

Empower Youth

Creating leadership opportunities for young people.

Our Vision

A beautiful future where every youth works together for society.

We imagine a Chauhag where no family is left without support, where every child studies under a working light, and where the hills we love stay green for generations after us.
