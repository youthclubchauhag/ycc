About Youth Club Chauhag

A non-profit youth organisation founded in 2025

Youth Club Chauhag is a non-profit, community-driven youth organisation established in 2025 by the energetic and dedicated youth of Village Chauhag, Post Office Maraog, Shimla, Himachal Pradesh.

Our club was founded with a simple vision — to unite the youth, protect nature, improve our village, preserve our culture, and serve society through volunteer work. Every positive action, no matter how small, helps build a better future for everyone.

Our Journey

Newly started, already making a difference

Although Youth Club Chauhag is a newly established organisation, we have already begun creating a positive impact in our village through environmental and community service activities. Our journey has just begun, and we remain committed to serving society with honesty, dedication, teamwork and responsibility.

What We Have Achieved

Meaningful work already underway

Mega Tree Plantation Drive

We successfully planted 1,500+ trees, including Devdar, Kalti and Mohru, to protect forests, improve biodiversity and create a healthier environment for future generations.

Village Cleanliness Campaign

Our volunteers cleaned roads and public areas, removed plastic waste, promoted awareness and encouraged youth participation to make Village Chauhag cleaner and greener.

Our Mission

The values that guide every activity
